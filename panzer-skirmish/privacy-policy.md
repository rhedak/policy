**Effective date:** 2026-07-08

This Privacy Policy describes how Rhedak ("we", "us") handles information in connection with **Panzer Island: Skirmish** (the "App"), a game that runs inside Reddit posts via Reddit's Devvit platform.

---

## Information we collect

The App runs entirely inside Reddit, using Reddit's own Devvit platform APIs. We do not operate any servers, accounts, analytics, or trackers of our own outside of what Devvit provides.

When you submit a score on a leaderboard, we store:

- Your Reddit username
- The stage, mode, and score you achieved

This data is stored in Reddit's Devvit Redis infrastructure, associated with the subreddit the app is installed in, and is used only to display per-stage leaderboards inside the App.

We do not collect your email address, IP address, device identifiers, or any other personal information. The App does not use cookies or third-party analytics.

Game progress itself (which units are unlocked, stage completion) is **not saved**. It resets every time you open the App. Only submitted leaderboard scores persist.

---

## Third-party services

The App is distributed through **Reddit**, via the Devvit platform. Reddit collects account and usage information under its own policies, independent of this App.

Reddit Privacy Policy: [reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy)

---

## Data retention and removal

Leaderboard entries persist for as long as the App remains installed on a subreddit. To request removal of your leaderboard entry, contact us using the details below, or delete your Reddit account, which removes your username's association with any stored score.

---

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It only stores the Reddit username and score of users who choose to play, which is scoped by Reddit's own age requirements for holding an account.
